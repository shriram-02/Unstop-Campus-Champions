<!-- Overview -->
<h2>📌 Overview</h2>

<p>
  The <strong>Unstop Campus Champions Program</strong> is a student-focused
  initiative centered on campus engagement, community building, and
  opportunities for students to connect with learning experiences.
  This repository documents my journey, promotional resources, creative
  assets, and learning associated with the Unstop Campus Champions
  initiative.
</p>

<p>
  The goal is to encourage student participation, share useful opportunities,
  strengthen campus communities, and develop communication, collaboration,
  and leadership skills through responsible student outreach.
</p>

<!-- Objectives -->
<h2>🎯 Objectives</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Build awareness of learning and career opportunities among students.</li>
  <li>Encourage participation in relevant student and professional events.</li>
  <li>Create engaging promotional content and visual resources.</li>
  <li>Strengthen communication, collaboration, and community-building skills.</li>
  <li>Document personal progress and lessons learned throughout the journey.</li>
</ul>
